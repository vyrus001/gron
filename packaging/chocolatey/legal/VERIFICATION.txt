The gron binary is downloaded during install from the vyrus001 fork release:

https://github.com/vyrus001/gron/releases/download/v0.8.1/gron_0.8.1_windows_amd64.zip

SHA-256: 9b8ad90f2a6459f20560bb1d18a1a8aeda52d5483a362a8f3192215f7238b250

The checksum matches the release's checksums.txt and can be checked with
Get-FileHash -Algorithm SHA256 on the downloaded archive. The ZIP contains
gron.exe at its root. The binary is not embedded in this package.
